Scope: prototipo/leva-app.html — MVP screens inside an iPhone frame with sidebar navigation, plus a separate 16:9 club TV view. Mode: Operate (app screens); the TV view leans Experience. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

Audience/job: players at the venue (next match, court, score, check-in), admin (paid/pending, draw, schedule), judge (score loading), public (TV).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every match is a LEVA fight poster. The app refuses the category default (white sports-SaaS cards, green "court" accents, flat lists of fixtures) and sets each pairing as a poster duel: two condensed italic names split by a gold slash, on deep navy-black.

OWN-WORLD: Ground #0A0D14 → #0E1626, shield navy #1B2A4A panels, metallic gold gradient (#8C6A2E→#D4AF5A→#F3DC9A) reserved for names, primary actions, winners and the slash. Archivo extra-condensed black italic for display; Archivo expanded light, wide-tracked caps for support lines. Chamfered corners on primary controls, gold hairlines, faint court-line drawing in backgrounds.

STORY: The player understands where and when they play and how it's going; believes this is LEVA's own stage; registers, pays, checks in and follows the match. Admin and judge get the same world at working density.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Inicio — "BIENVENIDO" tracked line, "JULIA" huge italic gold; directly below, the next-match poster duel (names / slash / CANCHA 3 / 17:30 / countdown) as the primary tap target; then the featured event poster; bottom tab bar.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction (no concept-seed roll; brand world pinned by user). Signature move: the gold slash duel. Signature motion: TV "llamado a cancha" slam-in and gold sweep on match end.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
